## 2025 — “Is it a bubble?”

_Capex, circular financing and the absence of demonstrated ROI. The argument is about whether the spending is justified._

### January 2025 · `T-2025-01`

**UNVERIFIED** — training knowledge, not source-checked

- **DeepSeek R1** released (20 Jan).
- **NVDA fell roughly 17% on 27 Jan**, about $590B of market capitalisation in one session — the largest single-day loss on record at the time.
- **Stargate** announced (21 Jan): $500B, OpenAI with SoftBank and Oracle.
- **OpenAI Operator**, its first browser agent.

**What the market was asking.** Does cheap Chinese training break the capex thesis? Is US model leadership a moat?

### February 2025 · `T-2025-02`

**UNVERIFIED** — training knowledge, not source-checked

## Early 2026 — “Does AI eat software?”

_The question moves from the infrastructure layer to the application layer. Agents are the mechanism; per-seat pricing is the thing at risk._

### January 2026 · `T-2026-01`

- **xAI launches Grok Business and Grok Enterprise.**[^1]
- **Anthropic releases Claude Cowork plugins** covering legal, sales, finance, marketing and support (30 Jan).[^2]
- Q4 SaaS earnings increasingly cite **the agent threat** by name.[^3]

**What the market was asking.** Do agents compress per-seat SaaS revenue? Which software categories are replaceable?

### February 2026 · `T-2026-02`

- **“SaaSpocalypse”** — about $285B of software market capitalisation lost in 48 hours from 3–4 Feb.[^2]
- **OpenAI Frontier** launches (5 Feb), a platform for managing AI agents.[^4]
- **Anthropic Series G at $380B** (12 Feb).[^5]
- **SpaceX acquires xAI at $250B.**[^6]

**What the market was asking.** Is the selloff structural or overdone? Will the model labs move up the stack into applications?

### March 2026 · `T-2026-03`

- **Microsoft announces Agent 365** and the **$99 Microsoft 365 E7** suite.[^7]
- **OpenAI raises $122B at $852B.**[^8]
- **Kore.ai** launches a cross-framework agent management platform.[^9]

**What the market was asking.** Who owns the agent control plane? Does bundling favour Microsoft?

## Mid 2026 — “Which software survives, and what are the labs worth?”

_Software capitulates and then recovers. The labs file to go public, which turns private marks into a public pricing question._

### April 2026 · `T-2026-04`

- **IGV troughs**, down about 37% from its peak.[^10]
- **Anthropic run-rate passes OpenAI** — about $30B against about $25B.[^11]
- **Claude Managed Agents** launches.[^12]
- **GPT-5.5** (23 Apr).[^11]

**What the market was asking.** Has Anthropic overtaken OpenAI in enterprise? Is software capitulation complete?

### May 2026 · `T-2026-05`

- **Agent 365 generally available at $15 per user**, with registry sync to AWS and Google.[^13]
- **Grok Build** coding agent.[^14]
- **Anthropic Series H at $965B.**[^8]

**What the market was asking.** Is about 15–20× run-rate justified? Is agent governance a standalone category or a feature?

### June 2026 · `T-2026-06`

- **Anthropic files confidentially for IPO** (1 Jun); **OpenAI files** (8 Jun).[^15]
- **Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 launch** (9 Jun); access suspended 12 Jun under Commerce export controls.
- **ADBE hits eight-year lows.**[^10]

**What the market was asking.** Can export controls reach frontier models themselves? What will the AI IPOs do to public multiples?

### July 2026 · `T-2026-07`

- **Commerce lifts the controls**; Fable and Mythos access restored 1 Jul.
- **xAI rebrands as SpaceXAI.**[^6]
- **Anthropic run-rate about $65B** by end-July.[^16]

**What the market was asking.** Is regulatory risk now priced into lab valuations? Is the growth durable?

### August 2026 · `T-2026-08`

- **Grok Bot** launches (11 Aug).[^17]
- **SpaceX S-1 shows the AI unit lost $2.47B in Q1 on $818M of revenue.**[^17]
- **SpaceXAI acquires Cursor.**[^6]
- **IGV turns positive** on the year.[^18]

**What the market was asking.** Can xAI convert to enterprise? Is rotation from chips into software beginning?

### September 2026 · `T-2026-09`

- **Grok Bot opens to enterprises** (3 Sep).[^19]
- **CRM order-value growth hits a four-year high**; TEAM rebounds.[^20]
- **IGV up more than 40% from the April lows.**[^21]
- **Microsoft Copilot Autopilot** runs on OpenClaw under Agent 365.[^22]

**What the market was asking.** Is the SaaSpocalypse over? Do agents create software demand rather than destroy it?

### October 2026 · `T-2026-10`

month still open

- **Anthropic IPO window reportedly September–October.**[^16]
- **Q3 earnings season begins.**

**What the market was asking.** How will the first lab IPO price? Does consumption pricing show up in Q3 numbers?

Understanding AI

AI explained for people who are numerate but have no machine-learning background.

Deck version v3.0 · 30 Aug 2026

Chapters are added as the subject moves, so this list grows. Each is one sitting of twenty to thirty minutes. Each chapter is a set of cards — one idea per card. Arrow keys move; every card has a Go deeper block, and an ask box that hands your question to Claude tagged with the card it came from.

The early chapters build on each other, so read them in order the first time. After that they stand alone as reference, and the numbering is for convenience rather than sequence — no chapter is the last one.

The AI market, month by month

A running chronology, not a chapter. It records what happened and what the market was arguing about at the time, so that a claim made in any chapter can be placed against the moment it was made. Entries are appended as months close; the earliest months will be revised as they are re-verified.

Provenance. January to December 2025 is drawn from training knowledge and has not been re-verified against sources — treat every date and figure in those months as indicative until checked. January 2026 onward is search-verified and carries numbered references. This split is marked on every month entry.
